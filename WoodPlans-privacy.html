Wood Plans

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Wood Plans (on the App Store as "Woodworking Plans: Cut List") is a woodworking plan app for iPhone. It is made by the independent developer named as the seller on its App Store page, based in Türkiye ("we"). We are the "controller" of the personal data the app handles. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. A photo goes to Google Gemini only after you agree, and the app checks with RevenueCat, under a random ID, whether Pro is active.

On your iPhone

When you save a project (Pro), Wood Plans keeps it on your iPhone, including its name, the choices you confirmed, your step progress and, if it has one, a small copy of its photo (at most 640 pixels). The app also keeps your settings there, such as units and your photo recognition choice. Plans are calculated on your iPhone.

None of this is sent to us, and we run no server of our own. Your iPhone backups include it, as for any app.

Photos and Google Gemini

Nothing is sent until you agree, on the notice shown with your first photo or with the switch in Settings. After that, each photo you take or choose goes straight from your iPhone to Google's Gemini API, an AI service that suggests the furniture type, rough size and material. You confirm or change them.

The app sends a copy of the photo, at most 1024 pixels on its longest side, with a fixed instruction text. The copy is redrawn, so it carries no location or camera details. No name, account or device identifier is sent. Like any internet request, Google also sees your IP address and basic technical details, such as the app's ID, iOS version and language, and handles them under its own privacy policy.

We use Gemini as a paid Google service. Under Google's terms for paid services, Google does not use your photo or its answer to improve its products. Google keeps them for up to 55 days to detect abuse and meet legal duties, and authorized Google staff may review a flagged request. Google may process this data in the United States and other countries. We keep no copy of your photo and cannot see or delete Google's copy. Google's terms: https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Saying no and withdrawing consent

Photo recognition is optional. Tap "Pick the type myself" on the notice and nothing is sent, or start from a template. Plans and Pro work the same without it.

To withdraw consent, go to Settings → Photos & AI in the app and turn off Read photos with Google Gemini. The app then sends no more photos and drops any answer still on its way. Photos already sent stay with Google for up to 55 days.

Camera, Photos and PDFs

Wood Plans uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo. It cannot see your photo library: iOS hands over only the photo you choose. Nothing is saved to your photo library.

A PDF you export (Pro) goes only where you send it from the iOS share sheet. If the plan started from your photo, the PDF shows that photo on its cover.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple sells and bills subscriptions under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We never see your payment details.

To check whether Pro is active and to count subscriptions, Wood Plans uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). When the app opens, it contacts RevenueCat with a random ID the app creates and technical details such as device model, iOS and app version, language and region, App Store country and the ID iOS gives our apps on your iPhone (IDFV). RevenueCat records when the app first and last connected. If you have or buy Pro, it also gets your App Store purchase records: plan, price, currency, dates and transaction IDs. It uses your IP address to estimate your country, then discards it. It never gets your name, email, photos or projects.

We see these records, under the random ID, in RevenueCat's dashboard. RevenueCat stores them in the United States and keeps them while we use its service, unless they are deleted. Its terms also let it use the data to improve its own service. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Sharing and transfers

We never sell your data or share it for advertising, and nobody tracks you across other companies' apps or websites through Wood Plans. Apart from RevenueCat's subscription records, the app has no analytics or advertising tools. With your permission in iPhone settings, Apple may also share anonymous crash and usage statistics with us.

Google (for the photo and its answer) and RevenueCat process data for us under data processing terms. These terms require them to keep it secure and to use it only as this policy describes. For data from the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google says it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses standard contractual clauses. RevenueCat uses standard contractual clauses. GitHub, which hosts this page, logs visitors' IP addresses for security.

Legal bases and your rights

We collect this data electronically, through the app. Photos go to Google only with your consent. RevenueCat records serve the Pro subscription you buy (a contract) and our legitimate interest in running the subscription and counting subscribers, which you can object to. Pro cannot work without them. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK or Türkiye, you can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to correct, delete, limit or transfer it. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We reply within 30 days, free of charge. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK). No decision with legal or similar effect is made about you automatically.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Projects and settings: on your iPhone until you delete a project (trash icon in Projects, then Delete) or the app. Older backups keep a copy until they are replaced or deleted.
  • Photos at Google: up to 55 days. We cannot delete them sooner.
  • RevenueCat records: first delete the app, or it will start a new record when it next opens. Then email us your App Store order ID, shown in Apple's receipt email and at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. We will delete the record and confirm by email. If you never subscribed, we cannot find your record, because it is stored under a random ID only.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to help you.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription in the app's Settings.

Children

Wood Plans is made for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used Wood Plans, contact us and we will delete any records we can find.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date. If what the app sends to Google changes, the app will ask for your consent again first. Questions or requests: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
