Privacy Policy — Wipe

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Wipe is an iPhone app that finds the reposts on your TikTok profile and removes the ones you choose.

Short version

Wipe has no server, no account and no analytics. Nothing you do in this app is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it. The app connects to one place only: TikTok.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

We do not operate a backend service. We do not use analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking software of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDKs.

What is stored on your device

When you sign in, TikTok issues session cookies to the browser view inside the app. Those cookies stay on your iPhone:

  • Session cookies are kept in the iOS Keychain, encrypted, marked this device only, so they are not included in iCloud or iTunes backups.
  • App preferences (for example the pacing measurement, and whether you have already run the single-repost check) are kept in standard app storage on the device.
  • Web data created by TikTok's own pages is kept in the app's browser storage, the same way Safari keeps cookies for a site you visit.

None of this leaves your iPhone. Tapping Disconnect in Settings deletes both the Keychain entry and the browser data.

Deleting the app removes everything above.

What Wipe does on TikTok

Using your own signed-in session, and only while you are using the app, Wipe does exactly two things:

  1. reads the list of reposts on your profile;
  2. removes the reposts you selected.

Wipe does not post, follow, unfollow, like, comment, send messages, change your profile, or read your private messages. Those capabilities do not exist in the app.

TikTok is a separate company

Wipe is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance. TikTok is a trademark of its owner.

When you sign in, you are on TikTok's own web page inside a browser view. We never see your password and have no technical way to read it. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy and terms.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports; those reports do not identify you.

Children

Wipe is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old, and old enough to hold a TikTok account in your country, to use this app.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control: Settings → Disconnect, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated version is published at this address before the change takes effect in the app.
