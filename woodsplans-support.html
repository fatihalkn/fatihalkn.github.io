Make a plan from a photo

Tap Take a photo or Choose from Photos. Get the whole piece in frame, from the front corner, in good light. The first time, the app asks before the photo goes to Google Gemini: Continue sends it, Pick the type myself sends nothing. Set the real size on Check the size, then tap Make my plan.

Photo not recognized?

  • Couldn't read this one: try again or use another photo, or pick one under Or pick the type.
  • You're offline, or Couldn't reach the photo reader: check your connection and tap Try again.
  • Photo reading is off right now: the reader is unavailable or refused this photo. Pick the type yourself; the plan is made the same way.

Start without a photo

Under Or start from a template, tap one of the eight types (Bookshelf, Floating shelf, Side table, Coffee table, Bench, Nightstand, Planter box, Workbench) and adjust its standard size. No photo is sent.

Units and saw kerf

Settings → Units: Inches (to 1/16 in) or mm; the default follows your region settings. Plans use standard US lumber sizes either way. Settings → Saw kerf: 1/16, 3/32, 1/8 (default) or 5/32 in. New plans use it; saved projects keep theirs.

Wood Plans Pro

Free for every plan, with no set limit: the 3D model, the part and lumber counts, the first two cut-list rows and the Plan checks. Pro (weekly or yearly, auto-renewing, no free trial) adds the rest: the full cut list, shopping list, cutting layout, hardware list, steps, PDF and saved projects. Family Sharing is not available.

Restore, cancel and refunds

  • Restore: with the same Apple Account, tap See the full plan, then Restore, or use the app's Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Cancel: the app's Settings → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. Help: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428
  • Refunds: only Apple can refund. Ask at https://reportaproblem.apple.com

Saved projects and PDFs

Save to Projects (Pro) keeps a plan on this iPhone and in its backups; nothing syncs. Touch and hold a project to rename it, or tap its trash button to delete it. Projects stay if Pro ends. Export PDF opens the share sheet to print or save the plan. If the plan started from your photo, the PDF shows that photo on its cover.

Check before you cut

A plan is a guide. Measure your boards, check every dimension before cutting and anchor tall furniture to a wall stud.

Privacy

Photos go to Google only after you agree. To withdraw, turn off the app's Settings → Photos & AI → Read photos with Google Gemini. Deleting the app deletes what it stored on your iPhone. The Privacy Policy explains what leaves your iPhone, who receives it and how to ask us to delete it.
